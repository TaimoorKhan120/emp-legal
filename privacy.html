Privacy Policy – Emergency Medicine Protocols

Last updated: 9 November 2026

"Emergency Medicine Protocols" (the app) is published by Hikayat AI Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we collect

  • Email address (optional): only if you enter it on the upgrade screen, so we can email your lifetime access code.
  • Anonymous user ID and push token: an anonymous identifier and a notification token, used to deliver the app's features and notifications.
  • Purchase information: the Google Play order ID of a purchase, used to unlock paid content. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never see your card details.
  • App activity: basic usage events (for example which protocols are opened) and your saved bookmarks and favourites, used to run and improve the app.

What we do not collect

We do not collect patient information, location, contacts, photos or files. The app is a clinical reference; please do not enter patient-identifiable data.

How we use it

To provide app functionality, unlock purchases, send notifications you have allowed, and understand usage so we can improve content.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for their own use. Service providers that host or deliver the app on our behalf (Google Firebase / Google Cloud and Google Play) process data under their own terms.

Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit. We keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Your choices and deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time: see how to request deletion.

Medical disclaimer

Content is for use by qualified healthcare professionals and does not replace clinical judgement or local protocols.
